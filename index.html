About

Built for the buyside. Open to every path in high finance.

TAIS trains members in the technical skills, investment judgment, and professional network that top firms look for. Most careers in alternatives start in investment banking, so we prepare members for the full path, from first internship to the buyside.

01

Train

A structured curriculum covering accounting, valuation, LBO modeling, and interview preparation.

02

Invest

Desk-based research and pitches across public and private markets, presented to members and industry judges.

03

Connect

Speaker events, firm visits, and an alumni network that links members to the firms where they want to work.

Divisions

Five desks, one society

Every member joins a desk, works on live research with a small team, and presents to the full society each semester.

Private Equity

How sponsors source, underwrite, and create value in companies.

  • LBO modeling
  • Deal case studies
  • PE case competitions

Public Markets and Equity Research

Fundamental research on public companies, from long/short pitches to sector coverage.

  • Long and short pitches
  • Sector coverage and earnings models
  • Semester research reports

Credit and Real Assets

Private credit, special situations, and real estate investing.

  • Direct lending analysis
  • Distressed case studies
  • Real estate underwriting

Investment Banking

The most common path into the buyside, and the core technical foundation.

  • M&A and valuation technicals
  • Mock interviews
  • Recruiting timelines

Asset Management

How long-only managers, endowments, and family offices build portfolios.

  • Portfolio construction
  • Manager selection and allocators
  • Wealth management and alternatives

Curriculum

What we cover

New members spend their first semester on technical foundations and recruiting prep. After that, members join a desk, and the whole society meets for sessions on markets and AI in finance.

Technical foundations

  • The three financial statements
  • DCF, trading comps, precedent transactions
  • LBO and M&A modeling
  • Reading filings and earnings calls

Private equity

  • Fund structure, fees, and carry
  • Buyouts, growth equity, venture, secondaries
  • Diligence and investment memos
  • Value creation and exits

Public markets and equity research

  • Long/short idea generation
  • Short selling and forensic accounting
  • Sector coverage and earnings notes
  • Hedge fund strategies

Credit and real assets

  • Direct lending and leveraged finance
  • Distressed debt and restructuring
  • Real estate underwriting
  • Infrastructure and energy

Investment banking

  • Bank types and group structure
  • Pitch books and deal processes
  • Sell-side and buy-side M&A
  • The path from banking to the buyside

Asset management

  • Long-only investing
  • Endowments, pensions, and family offices
  • Portfolio construction and allocation
  • Alternatives in wealth management

Markets and macro

  • Weekly market recaps
  • Rates, the Fed, and credit spreads
  • Deal activity and fundraising trends
  • Industry deep dives

Recruiting and careers

  • Recruiting timelines by track
  • Résumés and networking
  • Technical and behavioral interviews
  • Stock pitch and case interviews

Finance and AI

  • How funds use AI in research
  • Python and LLM tools for analysts
  • Introduction to quant strategies
  • Bloomberg, FactSet, and alternative data

Student-managed fund

The TAIS Fund

Desks pitch, the society votes, and every position is tracked against a benchmark. The fund starts as a paper portfolio so members build a real track record from day one.

Support the fund
Phase one

Paper portfolio

A full trade log and performance reported against the S&P 500.

Phase two

Real capital

Funding raised from alumni and firms to manage a live portfolio.

Investment policy

  • Liquid stocks and ETFs only
  • No options or margin
  • Position size limits
  • Every position approved by a society vote
  • Performance report published each semester

FAQ

Common questions

Still curious? Send us a message on Instagram or LinkedIn.

Do I need finance experience to apply?

No. We look for curiosity, work ethic, and genuine interest in investing. New members spend their first semester on technical foundations.

Who can apply?

University of Tennessee students interested in private equity, hedge funds, equity research, investment banking, or asset management.

What does the founding class do differently?

Founding members help build the society itself: setting up desks, shaping the curriculum, making the first fund pitches, and taking on leadership roles early.

What if I'm not sure which career path I want?

That's expected. The curriculum covers every major path in high finance, and members can explore several areas before settling on a desk.

How do I hear about information sessions?

Follow @tais_ut on Instagram and the society on LinkedIn. Every date is posted there first.
